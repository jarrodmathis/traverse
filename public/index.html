How to play

Three verses a day, each harder than the last. Type where you think each one is found, such as John 3:16, 1 Cor 13 or just Psalms.

  • Testament right: 10% of the verse's points
  • Book right: 50%. A near-miss book earns up to 30%
  • Chapter right: 80%. Close chapters earn partial credit
  • Verse exact: 100%. Close verses earn up to 98%

Verse 1 is worth 200, verse 2 is worth 300, verse 3 is worth 500. Everyone gets the same verses each day.
